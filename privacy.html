Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

This policy explains what information VeriSlí collects, how it is used, and the choices you have about it.

1. What information VeriSlí collects

VeriSlí collects the information you provide directly when using the app, along with limited technical information needed to keep the service working reliably.

2. Account information

When you create a VeriSlí account, we collect information such as your name, email address and farm account details. This is used to identify your account, keep your records secure and let you sign back in.

3. Farm records

VeriSlí stores the farm activity records you create, including entries such as sales, feeding, health and welfare, on-farm movement, breeding, births, losses, inspections and issues, fertiliser and pesticide use, and land actions, along with any dates, locations, subtypes and notes you add.

4. Photos and attachments

If you attach photos or other files to a record, those attachments are stored alongside the record so the supporting evidence stays with the relevant entry.

5. Device and technical information

We may collect basic technical information such as device type, operating system and app version to help keep VeriSlí working correctly and to diagnose problems.

6. How information is used

  • To provide and maintain the VeriSlí app and your account
  • To let you record, search and review your farm activity
  • To generate reports from your records
  • To sync your records across your devices when you are back online
  • To respond to support requests
  • To keep the service secure and reliable

7. Data storage

VeriSlí stores your account and farm records on secure third-party cloud infrastructure and syncs them to your devices through the app. We do not run our own physical servers; instead we rely on established hosting providers who are responsible for the physical and network security of the systems that store your data.

8. Service providers

VeriSlí uses third-party service providers to help operate the app, such as cloud hosting, data storage and infrastructure providers. These providers only process your information to provide services to VeriSlí and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

9. Data sharing

VeriSlí does not sell your personal information or farm records, and we do not share your information with advertisers. Information may be shared with the service providers described above, or where required by law, such as in response to a valid legal request.

10. Data retention

We retain your account and farm records for as long as your account remains active, so your history stays available to you. If you close your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal information within a reasonable period afterwards, except where we need to keep certain information for legal, accounting or dispute-resolution purposes.

11. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures designed to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. User rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your personal information. To make a request, contact us at the email address below.

13. Children

VeriSlí is not directed at children and is not intended for use by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will take reasonable steps to let users know.

15. Contact information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at verisli.support@gmail.com.
